## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is one long dinner table seen from above, running the length of the scroll; it refuses the category's full-bleed table photo with a serif headline and "Book now".
OWN-WORLD: The room is full-palette colour fields (aubergine, olive, marigold, rust) carrying the brand's organic line pattern tone-on-tone; down the centre runs a cream linen runner with a stitched hem. Paper objects (place card, menu card) sit on the linen; round photo slots are plates. Bodoni Moda capitals for headings, Jost petite-caps labels.
STORY: Visitors see the next seat waiting at the head of the table, book it in one tap, then walk the evening down the table (aperitivo, la mesa, sobretaula), meet the host, and find questions and private events at the foot.
